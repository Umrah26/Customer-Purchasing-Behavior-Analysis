# Customer-Purchasing-Behavior-Analysis
#### This Power BI dashboard  focuses on purchase amounts, product categories, customer ratings, shipping preferences, payment methods, product sizes, and previous purchase behavior. 
### Tools Used: Power BI (Power Query, Data Visualization), Excel.

## Dashboard Analysis:
## Business Value :
### The dashboard enables management to :
#### - Understand customer buying patterns.
#### - Improve inventory allocations.
#### - Optimize shipping strategies.
#### - Identify high-demand product categories.
#### - Monitor customer satisfaction through ratings.
## Business Insights -
### 1. Product Performance:
#### - Pants, Shoes, Jewelry and sweaters generate the highest purchase amounts.
#### - Shorts receive the highest average customer rating, indicating strong customer satisfaction.
### 2. Category Analysis :
#### - Clothing dominates overall purchases and represents the largest revenue- generating category.
#### - Accessories contribute a large share but remain below clothing sales.
### 3. Shipping Analysis :
#### - Free Shipping records the highest purchase value.
#### - Next-Day Air also shows strong performance, suggesting customers are willing to pay for faster delivery.
### 4. Customer Satisfaction :
#### - The average review rating is 3.75/5.
#### - Product ratings remain relatively consistent across categories, indicating stable customer experiences.
### 5. Size Preferences :
#### - Medium (M) size products generate the highest sales.
#### - Large (L) follows as the second most purchased size.
#### - Extra Large (XL) contributes the lowest purchase amount.
### 6. Payment Behavior :
#### - Customers actively use digital payment methods such as Paypal and Venmo alongside traditional payment options.

## Business Recommendations -
### 1. Inventory Optimization :
#### - Maintain higher inventory levels for Medium and Large-sized products due to higher demand.
#### - Increase stock availability for top-performing products such as Shoes, Pants and Jewelry.
### 2. Customer Experience :
#### - Investigate products with ratings below the dashboard average to improve quality and customer satisfaction.
#### - Collect customer feedback for lower-rated items.
### 3. Marketing Strategy :
#### - Focus promotional campaigns on high-performing categories such as Clothing and Accessories.
#### - Use personalized recommendations based on previous purchase behavior.
### 4. Shipping Strategy :
#### - Continue offering Free Shipping as it is associated with higher purchase volumes.
#### - Promote faster shipping options during peak sales periods.
### 5. Payment Strategy :
#### - Encourage digital payment methods through cashback offers and loyalty rewards.
#### - Analyze customer segments based on preferred payment  methods for targeted campaigns.
### 6. Revenue Growth : 
#### - Bundle complementary products from top-performing categories.
